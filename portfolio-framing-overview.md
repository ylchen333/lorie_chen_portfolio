# Portfolio Framing Overview: loriechen.com

*Written 8 Oct 2026. Based on a read of the live site (home, work, about, playlab), the creative technologist resume (`lorie_chen_resume_ct_2026_10.pdf`), `plans/portfolio-career-plan.md`, `plans/social-media-strategy.md` and `findings/human-3.0-assessment-2026-09-26.md`. I read page text only, so link targets and the interactive desk are unchecked.*

## The framing in one sentence

**Treat emerging computational techniques as creative materials, and build the interfaces that make them editable, so artists and institutions can shape what the technology does.**

The site already says most of this. The hero ("how new technology can become a more expressive, editable, and human creative medium"), the "Question:" format on every project, and the research-loop paragraph on About are the right foundation. This document sharpens it. It is not a repositioning.

## Who the site is for

| Priority | Audience | What they need to see |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Creative R&D and creative-tools teams (fal, ComfyUI, Specs at Snap, similar) | You can prototype, ship, and work with creatives and engineers |
| 2 | Art and tech funders, residencies, collaborators | You have a real point of view, not just a skill set |

The social media plan names an art audience as its goal, so Substack and Instagram carry the art voice. The site is the employer-facing home, and it should share one thesis with them. It should not copy their tone or posting rhythm.

## The through-line to make explicit

Gaussian splatting builds a scene from many partial views, and no single viewpoint holds the truth. Synth Splat, Catalogue Raisonné, Gem Stacking, The Long Way Home and Dead Things in Jars all ask how a capture reveals or distorts what is there. The site never says so. Two or three sentences on About or at the top of Work would turn five projects into one body of work and connect the site to your Substack and Instagram thesis.

## Tweaks, in priority order

### 1. State the through-line (small)
- Add 2–3 sentences on Work or About naming the partial-views idea in your own words.
- Keep the "research loop" paragraph. It already shows how you work.

### 2. Close the HCI/HRI gap (medium)
- You want to work in HCI and HRI, but Work has no such project. CoFRIDA appears only as one line in About.
- Add one entry, either CoFRIDA (robotic painting, the camera component, the IEEE RO-MAN 2024 demo) or the agentic-interface work from the Studio for Creative Inquiry.
- Use the same format as the other projects: a question, a method, a result.

### 3. Surface the ComfyUI and tools work (medium)
- Your resume lists custom ComfyUI nodes connecting 5+ models, real-time Gemini and ComfyUI interfaces used in CMU and RISD workshops, and 3DGS taught to 20+ students.
- None of this appears on Work, and it is the strongest evidence for your ComfyUI and fal targets.
- Add it as a project entry or as a clearly labelled section.

### 4. Add one outcome line per project (small)
Questions are good research framing, but skimmers need proof you finish things. Add one line each, using only what is true:
- **Live link.** Synth Splat (synthsplat.loriechen.com) and Catalogue Raisonné (room.loriechen.com) are deployed.
- **Partnerships and funding.** The Getty collaboration on Gem Stacking, and the partners and $1,000 in funding on Dead Things in Jars.
- **Audience.** Workshop use for the tools work.

### 5. Say what kind of team (small)
- "Looking for a creative R&D team" works as a start.
- Name the kind of team: creative tools, spatial computing, AI media tools. That helps the right readers recognize themselves.
- Keep the contact link visible without scrolling.

### 6. Keep your own voice (ongoing)
- Your best lines are specific ones: plotters doing "embarrassing amounts of work for small aesthetic payoffs," the Michigander and sci-fi paragraph.
- The polished hero line is the copy most worth putting in your own words.
- Your assessment flags AI-refined writing as a way to lose your voice. Use AI as a beta reader (point out problems), then fix them yourself.

### 7. Check the hero load (small)
- The Gaussian splat desk is a strong signal for the right audience, but it needs a capable device and takes time to load.
- Make sure the tagline, the work link and the contact link are readable before it finishes.
