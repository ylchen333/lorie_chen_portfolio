About

My practice moves through a research loop: find an emerging technique, ask what it might let creative people do, prototype a workflow, then follow its successes and interesting failures.

I’ve done research at the Studio for Creative Inquiry, Adobe Research and CMU’s Robotics Institute. I’m looking for a creative tools or spatial computing team.
